Altman’s UN Security Council remarks set out risks and three principles

OpenAI’s official Remarks as delivered page has an RSS timestamp of September 23 at 12:00 UTC and was opened. Issue 15 had covered advance reporting about a Wednesday briefing; this issue uses the delivered text. Altman describes a possible AI renaissance in creativity and discovery, alongside disruption on an industrial scale, and says he broadly agrees with Bengio.

The text identifies two bad paths, rather than three: losing human control over the future and concentrating power among a few people or countries. Its three principles are keeping human decision-making central, making scientific and economic benefits serve people, and empowering individuals as well as institutions. It calls for complementary national and international frontier standards and secure incident-reporting channels for governments, critical infrastructure and experts, while avoiding rules that entrench incumbents or a single business model.
