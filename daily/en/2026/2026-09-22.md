### Grok 4.7: xAI reports faster, cheaper coding and knowledge work

The xAI news page, dated September 21, describes Grok 4.7 as its strongest model for coding and knowledge work and claims twice the speed at half the price of comparable models. API prices start at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. A faster variant offers twice the output speed at twice the price. The article was opened; first discovery was September 25. Browser failures from September 19 to 21 kept this release out of Issue 15, so this is a late-discovered September 21 release rather than new September 22 news.

The page identifies the company as SpaceXAI while retaining the x.ai domain. It describes longer reinforcement-learning training aimed at tasks that take hours, a new safeguards stack, and the strongest refusal and jailbreak resistance the company has measured. These are company claims. Availability includes Cursor, Grok Build, the Grok API, third-party coding harnesses, model routers and cloud platforms. Its CursorBench button reports 46.3% for Grok 4.7 Extra High at an average $6.01 per task. Original source: https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7

<!-- item:featured-02 -->
### Claude Opus 5.5: Anthropic reports lower running costs and new safeguards

Anthropic calls Claude Opus 5.5 the first model in the Claude 5.5 family, matching Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing 40% less to run than Opus 5. The official page is dated September 22, with no publication time; it was opened and first discovered on September 25. This gives an official product-page follow-up to the previous issue’s Reuters report that Anthropic was weighing a release. The price and audit claims come from the product page.

Anthropic says this is its first release after calling for a slowdown at the frontier, with pre-release testing by external evaluators including Frontier Design and METR. It describes the model as its strongest on automated behavioral audits and says it carries safeguards designed for its most capable models. Examples include a tester migrating 680,000 lines of code in under a day and successful page-loading-time reductions in 39 of 40 web-app tasks. These examples and rankings are reported by Anthropic. Original source: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5
