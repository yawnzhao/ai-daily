Claude Opus 5.5: Anthropic reports lower running costs and new safeguards

Anthropic calls Claude Opus 5.5 the first model in the Claude 5.5 family, matching Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing 40% less to run than Opus 5. The official page is dated September 22, with no publication time; it was opened and first discovered on September 25. This gives an official product-page follow-up to the previous issue’s Reuters report that Anthropic was weighing a release. The price and audit claims come from the product page.

Anthropic says this is its first release after calling for a slowdown at the frontier, with pre-release testing by external evaluators including Frontier Design and METR. It describes the model as its strongest on automated behavioral audits and says it carries safeguards designed for its most capable models. Examples include a tester migrating 680,000 lines of code in under a day and successful page-loading-time reductions in 39 of 40 web-app tasks. These examples and rankings are reported by Anthropic. Original source: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5
